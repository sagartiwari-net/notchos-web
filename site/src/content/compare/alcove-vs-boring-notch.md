**Short answer:** install **Boring Notch** if it must be free and open source. Pay for **Alcove** if animation polish and the music HUD are the product. **NotchOS** (ours) is the third path when you want clipboard, tray, timers, teleprompter, and Android Share in one paid island.

Official sites: [Alcove](https://tryalcove.com/) · [Boring Notch](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch) · [NotchOS](/).

## Head-to-head

| | Alcove | Boring Notch | NotchOS |
|---|--------|--------------|---------|
| Price | ~$14.99 once | Free (OSS) | $15 / $3 mo |
| macOS (typical) | 15+ | 14+ | 26+ Apple silicon |
| Polish | Highest in class | Good / community | Commercial glass island |
| File tray | Often no | Yes / basic | Yes |
| Clipboard | Often no | Often no | Yes |
| Timers | Often no | Limited | Yes |
| Android Share | No | No | Yes |
| Trial | Check vendor | N/A | 7 days, no card |

## Alcove wins on polish

If the island’s motion and media presentation are why you are shopping, Alcove is the usual paid pick — provided your Mac meets their macOS requirement.

## Boring Notch wins on price and openness

Free, GPL-style licensing, and enough notch basics for many people. Read [Is Boring Notch safe?](/blog/is-boring-notch-safe) before you install any unsigned community build.

## Where NotchOS fits

Neither Alcove nor Boring Notch is trying to be a full productivity suite. NotchOS is: tray **plus** clipboard, calendar, tasks, timers, prompter, system stats, Android Share, and on-device writing. [Start trial](/trial).
