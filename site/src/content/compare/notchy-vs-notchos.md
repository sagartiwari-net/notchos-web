**Notchy** and **NotchOS** both turn the MacBook notch into a Dynamic Island-style workspace. This page is written by the NotchOS team — we disclose that up front. Verify Notchy’s current price and requirements on [notchy.dev](https://notchy.dev/).

## Quick verdict

- Prefer **Notchy** if you already like their feature mix, content ecosystem, and one-time license around their list price.  
- Prefer **NotchOS** if you want a lower lifetime price ($15) or $3/month, PayPal checkout, and first-class **Android Share** + on-device writing tools in the island.

## Pricing philosophy

NotchOS keeps Lifetime at a flat **$15** and Monthly at **$3**, with an optional Lifetime-only share coupon (max 20%). Notchy’s one-time price is typically higher — confirm on their pricing page.

## Try both if you can

Use each trial where available. Keep the app whose daily faces you actually open.
