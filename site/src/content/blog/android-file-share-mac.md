**Android file transfer to Mac** usually means USB, cloud folders, or Nearby Share–style flows. If you already use a notch app all day, starting the handoff from the island saves a trip to another window.

## NotchOS Android Share

NotchOS includes an **Android Share** face (Pro): send files toward your phone with **Nearby / QR + PIN** style flows from the notch. Open it from the Tools grid or the island tab strip.
