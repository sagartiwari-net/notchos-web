**MacBook Air notch apps** are the same Dynamic Island category as on MacBook Pro — the Air’s camera housing is still a notch third-party apps can expand.

## What to check on Air

1. **Apple silicon** — NotchOS requires Apple silicon (M1 Air and later generations that ship a notch, depending on model year).  
2. **macOS version** — NotchOS needs macOS 26 or later. Many open-source notch apps run on macOS 14, and Alcove on 15, so check this first if your Air is on an older macOS.  
3. **Physical notch** — most islands expect the built-in camera cutout; external displays are a separate story.
