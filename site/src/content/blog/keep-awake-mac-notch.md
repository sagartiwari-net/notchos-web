**Keep Mac awake** utilities (classic “Caffeine” style) stop sleep while you present, transfer files, or wait on a long job. NotchOS puts **Keep Awake** next to Pomodoro / Countdown / Stopwatch on the Timers face — presets like 30m, 1h, 2h, 4h, or until off.

## Why the notch

You already glance at the top of the display. Toggling awake state there beats hunting a menu-bar cup icon in a forest of icons.
