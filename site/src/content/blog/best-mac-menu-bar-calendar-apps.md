**Mac menu bar calendar** apps (Itsycal-style) put the month one click from the clock. Notch apps put **today’s agenda under the camera** instead.

## Menu bar vs notch

| | Menu bar calendar | Notch calendar face |
|---|-------------------|---------------------|
| Position | Near the clock | Under the notch |
| Best for | Classic Mac muscle memory | Island + live “next meeting” chips |
| Stacking | Another icon | Shares island with tray/timers |

## NotchOS

NotchOS **Calendar** shows today and the week in the island, alongside tasks and home overview. [Preview](/?face=calendar) · [Trial](/trial)

If you love a dedicated menu-bar month grid, keep it. If you want calendar **with** the rest of a Dynamic Island, try NotchOS.
