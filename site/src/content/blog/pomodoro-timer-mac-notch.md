A **timer app for Mac** is easy to find; a timer that stays visible when you are deep in another app is harder. Menu-bar timers help. Notch apps can show remaining time on the closed island.

## NotchOS Timers

NotchOS ships **Pomodoro**, **Countdown**, and **Stopwatch** on one Timers face, plus **Keep Awake** presets so the Mac does not sleep mid-session. When a timer runs, the closed notch can still show the time.

## Pomodoro basics (still useful)

1. Work block (often 25 minutes)  
2. Short break  
3. Longer break after a few cycles  

NotchOS does not invent productivity science — it just keeps the clock where your eyes already glance: the top of the display.
