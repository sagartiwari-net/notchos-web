A **teleprompter app for Mac** keeps your script scrolling while you look at the lens — useful for YouTube, courses, and meeting intros.

## What matters

- Scroll speed (WPM) you can adjust live  
- Font size / line spacing / column width  
- Mirror mode for physical glass teleprompters  
- Placement that does not fight the camera  

## NotchOS Prompter

NotchOS puts the **Prompter** in the notch: library of scripts, editor, and reading controls (speed, size, spacing, width, alignment). The script sits under the camera housing so eye-line stays natural. Start reading from the island; stop when you are done.

## Alternatives

Dedicated teleprompter apps (and some presentation tools) exist if you need multi-display show control. For creators who already live in a notch island, bundling the prompter with clipboard and timers reduces app hopping.
