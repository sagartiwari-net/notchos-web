Searching for a **clipboard manager for Mac** usually means you want more than ⌘V — a history of recent copies, images, and snippets you can paste again.

## Built-in vs dedicated

macOS has added more pasteboard conveniences over time (version-dependent). Dedicated managers still win when you need searchable history pinned near your workflow. Menu-bar clipboard apps are common; notch apps put history under the camera instead.

## What NotchOS does

**NotchOS** includes a **Clipboard** face in the island: recent items, quick paste/clear actions, and a layout that matches the rest of the notch tools. It is part of the same app as tray, timers, and calendar — not a separate clipboard utility.

## When to use a standalone clipboard app

Use a specialist if you need advanced rules, cloud sync across devices, or deep formatting tools beyond what an island UI should hold. Use NotchOS if you want clipboard history **next to** files, timers, and the rest of your notch tools.
