Two philosophies:

1. **Hide the notch** — wallpaper / menu-bar painting apps make the cutout less obvious.  
2. **Use the notch** — Dynamic Island apps turn the dead zone into controls.

## Hide

Good when you hate the visual break and do not want new UI. You gain camouflage, not features.

## Use (NotchOS and peers)

Good when you want clipboard, tray, timers, music, or Android Share without more menu-bar icons. See [How to use the MacBook notch](/blog/how-to-use-macbook-notch) and [Best Mac notch apps](/best-mac-notch-apps).

**NotchOS** is firmly in the “put it to work” camp. [Trial](/trial)
