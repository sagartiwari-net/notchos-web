A **clipboard manager for Mac** keeps what you copy so you can paste it again. This roundup separates **menu-bar specialists** from **notch-integrated history**. No fake star ratings.

## Approaches

| Approach | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| Built-in macOS paste tools | Free, simple | Limited history UX |
| Menu-bar clipboard apps | Deep search, rules, sync | Another always-on icon |
| Notch clipboard face | History next to tray/timers | Island UI, not a power-user database |

## NotchOS

NotchOS includes **Clipboard** in the Dynamic Island — history beside tray, timers, and the rest of your faces. Best when you already want a Mac notch app. [Clipboard guide](/blog/clipboard-manager-mac) · [Trial](/trial)

## When to buy a specialist

Choose a dedicated clipboard manager if you need cloud sync across iPhone/Mac fleets, OCR pipelines, or heavy snippet libraries. Pairing a specialist **with** a notch app is fine; just avoid three apps fighting the same pasteboard.
