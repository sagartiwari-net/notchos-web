If you need a **free Dynamic Island for Mac**, start with apps that stay free after install — not just a trial. This page separates “forever free” from “try then pay,” and notes where **NotchOS** sits (we build it).

## Short answer

- **Truly free (open source):** [Boring Notch](/alternatives/boring-notch) is the usual recommendation for a no-cost Mac notch island. Confirm the current macOS minimum on its GitHub/releases page.  
- **Free to try:** NotchOS (7 days, no card), and several paid apps offer short trials. After that you pay.  
- **Not free:** Alcove, NotchNook, Notchy, DynamicLake — paid licenses (sometimes with a trial).

## Comparison

| App | Really free? | Notes |
|-----|--------------|--------|
| Boring Notch | Yes — open source | Music-first; thinner feature set |
| NotchOS | Trial only | 7 days, then $15 lifetime or $3/mo |
| Notchy | Trial then paid | One-time license after trial |
| Alcove | Paid | Polish-focused; check macOS floor |
| NotchNook | Paid | Tray + design; one-time or monthly |
| DynamicLake | Paid | Confirm vendor pricing |

## When free is enough

Choose free if you mainly want Now Playing on the notch, you are fine with open-source polish, and you do not need clipboard history, a teleprompter, Android Share, or AI writing in the island.

## When a paid island is worth it

Pay once (or monthly) if you want **one** app for tray + clipboard + timers + calendar + more. That is the NotchOS pitch: put the Mac’s notch to work for the tools you already open all day.
