**Spotify controls in the MacBook notch** (and Apple Music / other players) are one of the first reasons people install a Dynamic Island app for Mac.

## What you want

- Play / pause / skip without bringing the player forward  
- Artwork and track title at a glance  
- Optional lyrics if you use them  

## NotchOS

NotchOS Home / media faces surface **Now Playing** in the island so media stays with your other tools (clipboard, timers, tray). Exact player support depends on macOS media APIs and the apps you run — try your stack in the [trial](/trial).

Media-first apps like [Alcove](/alternatives/alcove) specialize in polish; NotchOS optimizes for **media plus productivity** in one place.
