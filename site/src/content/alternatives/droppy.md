**Droppy** is a paid Mac utility focused on a **file tray / shelf** experience near the notch, with clipboard and extension-style extras depending on version. Confirm current pricing on the [vendor site](https://droppy.app/) (URL may redirect — use their live homepage).

**NotchOS** is our product. Unaffiliated with Droppy.

## Strengths

- File-drop workflow as the product center  
- Smaller one-time price than some full islands  
- Useful if you only want shelf + light extras  

## Gaps

- Not always a full Dynamic Island with timers, teleprompter, Android Share, and AI writing  
- You may stack another app for music HUD or system vitals
