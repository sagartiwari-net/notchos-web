**MediaMate** replaces stock macOS volume and brightness HUDs with compact overlays and can show **Now Playing** near the notch. Official overview: [MediaMate site](https://wouter01.github.io/MediaMate/).

**NotchOS** is our product. Unaffiliated with MediaMate.

## Strengths

- Purpose-built HUD replacement (volume / brightness / keyboard brightness)  
- Works on many Macs, not only “full island” workflows  
- Light footprint if you only want better system overlays  

## Gaps vs a full notch suite

- Not a full Dynamic Island with tray, clipboard history, teleprompter, Android Share, or AI writing  
- You may still want a separate notch app for productivity faces  

## MediaMate vs NotchOS

| | MediaMate | NotchOS |
|---|-----------|---------|
| Focus | HUDs + Now Playing | Full productivity island |
| Volume / brightness HUD | Yes (core) | Not the primary pitch |
| Clipboard / tray / timers / prompter | No | Yes |
| Android Share | No | Yes (Pro) |
| Trial | Check vendor | 7 days, no card |
| Price | Paid utility | $15 lifetime / $3 mo |

## Where NotchOS fits

Keep MediaMate if HUD polish is the whole job. Choose NotchOS if you want the **Mac notch** to host files, clipboard, timers, calendar, teleprompter, and Android Share — [trial](/trial).
