**Boring Notch** is a free, open-source Mac notch / Dynamic Island app. It is the default answer when people search for a free island on the MacBook notch. Confirm install steps and the current macOS floor on the [official repository](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch).

**NotchOS** is our paid product (7-day trial). We are not affiliated with Boring Notch.

## Strengths

- Free and auditable (open source)  
- Solid music / notch basics without a subscription  
- Active community interest around “free Dynamic Island for Mac”

## Gaps (vs a full productivity island)

- Narrower tool surface than paid suites (clipboard, teleprompter, Android Share, AI writing vary by app)  
- Polish and packaging differ from commercial products
