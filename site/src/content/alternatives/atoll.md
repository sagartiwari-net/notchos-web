**Atoll** is a free, open-source Mac notch / Dynamic Island app (GPL-3.0). It builds a focused island for media, live activities, stats, timers, clipboard, and more. Source and releases: [Ebullioscopic/Atoll](https://github.com/Ebullioscopic/Atoll/).

**NotchOS** is our paid product. Unaffiliated with Atoll.

## Strengths

- Free and open source  
- Live Activities / lock-screen widget direction on newer macOS  
- Media, shelf-style workflows, clipboard and timers in one OSS project  

## Gaps

- Requires a **physical notch** and typically **macOS 14+**  
- Community polish vs commercial packaging differs  
- Android Nearby Share and on-device writing suite are NotchOS Pro strengths

## Where NotchOS fits

Install Atoll if you want a **free OSS** island and your Mac meets their notch + macOS floor. Choose NotchOS for a signed commercial build with **Android Share**, teleprompter, Keep Awake, and PayPal licensing — [trial](/trial).
