**Alcove** is a paid Mac Dynamic Island / notch app known for animation polish and a strong music HUD. Official site: [tryalcove.com](https://tryalcove.com/).

**NotchOS** is our product. We are not affiliated with Alcove. Prices and OS floors change — verify on Alcove’s site before you buy.

## Strengths

- High-end island motion and media presentation  
- Clear product focus (do fewer things, do them beautifully)  
- Strong brand search demand (`alcove mac`, `dynamic island for mac`)

## Gaps

- Needs a recent macOS (so does NotchOS, which requires macOS 26)  
- Feature surface is often narrower than full productivity suites (clipboard, tray, timers, Android Share)
